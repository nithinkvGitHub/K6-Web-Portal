📖 How to Deploy on Your 2 Office EC2 Instances

This exact architecture works on your laptop today (using local ports 4101 and 4102) and deploys onto your 2 office EC2 machines in 3 steps:

  1. On EC2 Instance #1 & EC2 Instance #2: Start the lightweight Worker Agent:
    PORT=4101 node server/worker-agent.mjs
  2. Point to Your Office Framework: Set Framework Workspace Root on the left to:
    c:\env_qa\perftest-framework-active\ai-agent-perf-framework
  3. Update Worker URLs: Replace 127.0.0.1:4101 and 127.0.0.1:4102 with your 2 EC2 Private IPs (e.g. http://10.24.1.101:4101 and http://10.24.2.102:4102).
